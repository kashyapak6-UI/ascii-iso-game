# ISO REEF

An isometric ocean game drawn entirely with colored ASCII characters.
You are a clownfish on a coral reef.

**Play:** [https://kashyapak6-ui.github.io/ascii-iso-game/](https://kashyapak6-ui.github.io/ascii-iso-game/)

## How to play

Collect every golden bite (`*` / `O`) on the reef, then swim into the
anemone to clear the level. Jellyfish and sharks will take a life if they
touch you. You have three lives.

Later reefs get larger, with more food and faster hunters.

## Controls

- **Laptop:** arrow keys or WASD
- **Phone:** on-screen D-pad, or swipe the reef
- Directions are screen-space: up moves toward the top of the screen

## Notes

Single HTML file, no libraries or assets. Open `index.html` locally if you
want to play offline.
